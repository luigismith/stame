The case · draft 4 · October 2026

A birch console that holds all three.

The MPK mini IV on the left under an engraved black acrylic mask, the macro pad on the right in line with the keys, the Miyoo Flip above it on a shelf with the screen at 65°. 589 × 244 mm, 46 mm to the shelf. The cables run behind and under the shelf to a USB hub hidden in the tower.

Assembly

Nine steps, tabs in slots and glue.

No screws in sight: the back strip, the divider and the tower walls lock into the base with tabs, the sides are four layers of 4 mm birch. In amber, the parts each step adds.

The parts

Five sheets for the laser, six parts for the printer, one sheet of paper.

Sheets of 390 × 390 for an AtomStack S10, nested by material; red is cut, brown is engraved. The sides and the mask carry the same dot lettering as the screen.

Print · PETG

The Flip's cradle and visor

A low tray with one cut-out for the jack and the USB-C, open at the back and at the shoulders; a visor that follows the screen at 65°.

Print · PETG

Frame, holder, brackets

The frame that holds the pad on the base, the hub holder, six M3 brackets between base and sides.

Print · TPU

Eleven feet

Under the mask, where the paper template marks them: flat spots of the MPK, away from every opening.

Bought

Clear keycaps, hub, screws

Fifteen clear two-layer keycaps for Cherry MX, a plain USB 2.0 hub without Power Delivery, M3 × 10 screws and nuts, 3 × 12 wood screws, six rubber feet.

Before cutting

Measured from photographs, checked on paper.

Every measurement lives in one file, misure.py, with its source: [M] manual or datasheet, [O] observed, [D] deduced, [V] to verify. The MPK's controls were measured on a photo from above whose scale holds on both axes, to about a millimetre; the macro pad on a photo where its standard 18 mm keycaps are the ruler, confirmed by a button of the MPK in the same frame; the Flip's ports on two photos that agree within half a millimetre.

Building the 3D model from the parts found real mistakes before any wood was cut: walls overlapping the shelf, screws outside the walls, a cradle covering the shoulder buttons, holes on the mask overlapping, and the engraving on the sides coming out mirrored. Now the generator refuses an engraved part that would read backwards.

The three checks left

  • Print the mask's template at 100 %, check the 100 mm ruler, lay it on the MPK.
  • Cut a test piece to set the laser and the play of the tabs (0.2 mm a side).
  • Check the heights: the pad, the MPK's small buttons, how far the Flip opens (it needs 115°).
